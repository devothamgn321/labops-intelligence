How the operating system works

The goal is not a prettier spreadsheet. It is a repeatable decision system that links demand signals to execution and governance.

1. Forecast demand

Start with current headcount, growth assumptions, seat ratios, specialized bench needs, and a planning buffer. Project monthly requirements over 24 months.

2. Model real capacity

Reduce nominal capacity for downtime and only release expansion capacity when the modeled completion date occurs. Track seats and benches separately.

3. Drive portfolio decisions

Connect shortfalls to project timing, risk, funding, approvals, and dependencies. Route red items to executive triage and document decisions with owners.
